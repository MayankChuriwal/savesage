Flipkart Freedom Sale 2026: SBI Credit Card Offers, 10% Discount & Savings Guide

The Flipkart Freedom Sale 2026 is live from 8 August to 16 August, with offers across mobiles, electronics, fashion, groceries, home appliances and more. Flipkart Plus and BLACK members get early access from 7 August.

11 Aug 2026

If you have an eligible SBI Credit Card, you can get 10% off on qualifying purchases, with the base discount going up to ₹1,500. You can also get additional Bonus Offers, taking your total savings to ₹6,250 per card.

But there is a catch. This isn't a flat 10% discount on everything you buy. The minimum purchase value, maximum discount and eligibility can change depending on the category, product and payment method.

Get 10% Instant Discount with SBI Credit Cards

The main SBI offer is a 10% Instant Discount on eligible products. However, how much you actually save depends on what you're buying and whether you pay normally or choose EMI.

For example, if you're buying a mobile worth ₹4,990 or more, you can get up to ₹750 off with a regular Credit Card transaction or up to ₹1,250 off with an eligible EMI transaction.

You can make multiple eligible transactions during the offer period, but the Base Offer discount is capped at ₹1,500 per Credit Card for each applicable sale period. The first period runs from 8 August to 10 August 2026 and the second from 11 August to 16 August 2026. Flipkart Plus and BLACK members get access from 7 August. 

The discount is calculated on the Net Cart Value (NCV). In simple terms, this is the value of the eligible products after applicable discounts and exchange offer redemptions.

The maximum Bonus Offer discount is ₹4,750 per Credit Card during the sale. If your purchase qualifies for both offers, you can get the 10% Instant Discount plus the applicable Bonus Offer in the same transaction.

Which SBI Credit Cards Are Eligible? 

Not every SBI Credit Card is eligible for this offer.

The following are excluded:

  • SBI Corporate Credit Cards

  • Flipkart SBI Co-branded Credit Card

  • SBI Cashback Card

  • Paytm SBI Credit Card

The offer is available only on eligible products and cards, so don't assume that every product qualifies just because you have an eligible SBI Credit Card. Check the bank offer section on the product page before buying, then enter your card details at checkout and make sure the discount appears in the final payment summary. 

This is particularly important for the Bonus Offers, as spending ₹39,990 or more does not automatically mean you'll get the corresponding bonus discount because these offers are available only on select products.

Important Terms & Conditions

A few additional terms from the offer T&C are worth knowing:

  • Reward Points earned on offer-eligible transactions may be forfeited under SBI Card's Rewards Terms & Conditions. For Instant Discount offers, the forfeiture may take place within 120 days of the end of the offer month.

  • Cancelled, returned, disputed or unauthorised transactions will not qualify for the offer. If part of an order is cancelled or returned, eligibility will be calculated on the net amount paid after the cancellation or return. 

  • SBI Card and Flipkart can modify, extend, withdraw or replace the offer as permitted under the applicable terms.

  • For purchases of ₹2,00,000 or more, PAN submission may be required within four days of the purchase. The order may be cancelled if the required PAN details aren't submitted or the name doesn't match.

  • The offer is valid only in India and the minimum age requirement is 18 years.
