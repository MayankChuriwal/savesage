IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Cards Get Double Welcome Benefits Till 31 July 2026

Planning to apply for an IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card? For a limited time, Axis Bank has doubled the welcome benefits on both the IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card and the IndiGo Axis Bank Premium Credit Card.

21 Jul 2026

Apply by 31 July 2026, pay the applicable joining fee and receive up to 10,000 IndiGo BluChips, depending on the card you choose. Unlike some recent airline credit card offers, there's no minimum spend requirement to unlock the enhanced welcome benefits. 

What's the Offer?

New applicants can receive double the usual welcome BluChips on both IndiGo co-branded credit cards during the offer period between 1 June and 31 July 2026.

How to Get the Double Welcome Benefits

To receive the enhanced welcome benefits, you need to:

  • Apply for either the IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card or the IndiGo Axis Bank Premium Credit Card by 31 July 2026. 

  • Get your application approved

  • Pay the applicable joining fee after your card is issued. 

Unlike some recent airline credit card promotions, there is no minimum spend requirement to earn the additional BluChips.

The welcome benefits are credited in two stages:

IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card

  • Within around 30 days: 1,200 IndiGo BluChips

  • Within 60 days: Additional 1,200 IndiGo BluChips + 1 6E Eats Voucher

IndiGo Axis Bank Premium Credit Card

  • Within around 30 days: 5,000 IndiGo BluChips + 1 6E Eats Voucher

  • Within 60 days: Additional 5,000 IndiGo BluChips

IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card Comparison 

While both cards qualify for the limited-time welcome offer, they are designed for different types of travellers. The Standard card offers a lower joining fee, while the Premium card provides higher reward rates, more lounge access and larger milestone benefits. Here's a side-by-side comparison.

Any rewards earned beyond this cap will not count towards annual milestone spend calculations.

Important Offer Terms 

Before applying, keep these conditions in mind:

  • Offer applies only to eligible applications submitted by 31 July 2026

  • Joining fee payment is mandatory

  • Offer benefits are not applicable on Lifetime Free or First Year Free cards. 

  • Welcome benefits are issued as IndiGo BluChips Vouchers

  • Promotional BluChips are credited separately from the standard welcome benefit

  • Vouchers remain valid for 6 months from the date of issue

  • No extension or reissuance is available after expiry

  • Standard IndiGo BluChip Programme Terms and Axis Bank credit card terms continue to apply

Should You Apply?

If you regularly travel with IndiGo airlines, the IndiGo Axis Bank Premium Credit Card offers better long-term value through higher reward rates, airport lounge access, milestone rewards, annual renewal benefits and a monthly BookMyShow Buy One Get One offer. 

If you fly only a few times a year, the IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card offers a lower joining fee, rewards on eligible UPI spends and double the usual welcome BluChips plus a complimentary 6E Eats Voucher during the promotional period. 

If you were already planning to apply for an IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card, this limited-period promotion offers better first-year value without requiring any additional spending. Just make sure you apply by 31 July 2026 and pay the joining fee to qualify for the enhanced welcome benefits.

FAQs

1. When does the IndiGo Axis Bank double welcome benefits offer end? 

The offer is available for eligible IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card and IndiGo Axis Bank Premium Credit Card applications submitted up to 31 July 2026. 

2. Is there any spending requirement to get the double welcome BluChips? 

No. Unlike some recent airline credit card offers, there is no minimum spend requirement. You only need to pay the applicable joining fee to qualify.

3. What welcome benefits will I receive under this offer? 

During the offer period:

  • IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card: 2,400 IndiGo BluChips + 1 6E Eats Voucher instead of 1,200.

  • IndiGo Axis Bank Premium Credit Card: 10,000 IndiGo BluChips + a 6E Eats Voucher instead of 5,000 BluChips + a 6E Eats Voucher.

Lifetime free and First year free card members will not be eligible for Welcome/Renewal benefits vouchers 

4. When will I receive the welcome BluChips?

The welcome benefits are credited in two stages. The regular welcome benefit is generally issued within 30 days of joining fee payment, while the promotional BluChips are usually credited within 60 days. 

5. Which IndiGo Axis Bank Credit Card should I choose?

The Standard card is better suited for occasional IndiGo travellers who want a lower joining fee, while the Premium card is designed for frequent travellers seeking higher reward rates, airport lounge access and larger milestone benefits.
