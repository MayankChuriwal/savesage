Axis Atlas Credit Card Review

When it comes to popular credit cards, cashback cards usually steal the spotlight. However, for frequent travellers, cards that earn air miles can often deliver much better long-term value. The Axis Atlas Credit Card is designed for users who prefer earning EDGE Miles that can be transferred to airline and hotel loyalty programmes instead of receiving flat cashback.

1 Aug 2026

Unlike regular rewards cards, the Axis Atlas Credit Card earns a dedicated travel currency called EDGE Miles. These miles can be redeemed through Axis Bank TRAVEL EDGE or transferred to multiple airline and hotel loyalty programmes, making the card particularly appealing for users who travel regularly.

Along with EDGE Miles, the card offers tier-based rewards, milestone benefits and complimentary airport lounge access. With a joining fee of ₹5,000 plus GST, the Atlas Credit Card is positioned as a premium travel rewards card for users with moderate to high annual spending.

If you're unfamiliar with airline miles or hotel points, check out our Travel Loyalty Programs Explained guide to understand how these programmes help you earn free flights and hotel stays. 

Eligibility Criteria

The Axis Atlas Credit Card is no longer being issued to new applicants. The eligibility criteria below applied when the card was available for application and remain relevant for existing cardholders.

  • Primary cardholder must be between 18 and 70 years of age.

  • Meet the income thresholds:

    • Salaried professionals: Net annual income of ₹12 lakhs or more.

    • Self-employed individuals: Net annual income of ₹15 lakhs or more.

  • Must be a resident of India.

EDGE Miles Transfer Program

EDGE Miles earned on the Axis Atlas Credit Card can be redeemed in two main ways:

1. Axis Bank TRAVEL EDGE

EDGE Miles can be used to book flights and hotels through Axis Bank TRAVEL EDGE at a value of ₹1 per EDGE Mile. The overall transfer limit is 1,50,000 EDGE Miles per customer ID in a calendar year. 

2. Airline & Hotel Partners

EDGE Miles can also be transferred to participating airline and hotel loyalty programmes. The transfer ratio varies depending on the partner programme.

Does the Axis Atlas Credit Card Still Make Sense?

The Axis Atlas Credit Card remains one of Axis Bank's strongest travel-focused credit cards, offering accelerated EDGE Miles on travel bookings, access to airline and hotel transfer partners, milestone rewards and tier-based airport lounge access. While it is no longer being issued to new customers, existing cardholders can continue to enjoy these benefits as per the applicable terms and conditions.

For existing users who travel frequently and spend at least ₹7.5 lakhs annually, the card can continue to deliver good value through its rewards programme and membership benefits. If you're looking for a new travel credit card, however, you'll need to consider other options currently available from Axis Bank or other issuers.

FAQs

1. Why is the Axis Atlas Credit Card no longer available for new applicants?

Axis Bank has stopped issuing the Axis Atlas Credit Card to new customers. Existing cardholders can continue using the card and enjoy its rewards, lounge access and EDGE Miles benefits according to the applicable terms and conditions. If you're looking for a new travel credit card, you'll need to consider other options currently offered by Axis Bank or other issuers.

2. What is the best way to redeem EDGE Miles?

EDGE Miles can be redeemed through Axis Bank TRAVEL EDGE for flight and hotel bookings or transferred to eligible airline and hotel loyalty programmes. The best redemption option depends on your travel plans, preferred loyalty programme and the applicable transfer ratio at the time of redemption.

3. Which transactions do not earn EDGE Miles on the Axis Atlas Credit Card?

The Axis Atlas Credit Card does not award EDGE Miles on spends made towards rent, fuel, insurance, utilities, telecom, wallet loading, government payments and gold or jewellery purchases. These categories are also excluded from milestone benefit calculations.

4. Can I transfer EDGE Miles to airline and hotel loyalty programmes anytime?

Yes. EDGE Miles can generally be transferred whenever you have the minimum required balance, subject to the partner's transfer ratio, annual transfer limits and the latest terms published by Axis Bank. Once a transfer request is processed, it usually cannot be reversed.

5. Is the Axis Atlas Credit Card better than a cashback credit card?

It depends on how you use your credit card. Frequent travellers who regularly book flights and hotels may find more value in the Axis Atlas Credit Card because of its travel rewards and transfer partners. Users who prefer straightforward savings without managing loyalty programmes may find a cashback credit card more suitable.
