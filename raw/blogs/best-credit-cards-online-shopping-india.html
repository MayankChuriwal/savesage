Things to Know Before Choosing an Online Shopping Credit Card

The best online shopping credit card depends on where you shop, how much you spend each month and how you prefer to earn rewards. Before applying, consider these factors. 

Shop Where You Spend the Most. If your spending is spread across multiple websites, a general cashback card is often a better choice. 

Check the cashback cap. A card advertising 10% cashback may still earn less than a card offering 5% cashback if the monthly reward cap is too low. Always compare the maximum cashback you can actually earn. 

Check the exclusions. Categories such as utilities, rent, gift cards and wallet loading often do not earn cashback.

Consider the annual fee. The cashback you earn over a year should comfortably offset the annual fee. 

Understand how rewards are credited. Direct cashback is generally simpler than reward points or Jewels that require redemption. 

PhonePe SBI Card SELECT BLACK and Jupiter Edge+ can offer higher rewards on paper but they're also more restrictive. You'll need to keep track of eligible merchants, reward caps and redemption rules to get the full value.

1. SBI Cashback Card

If your online shopping is spread across different websites instead of just one, SBI Cashback is probably the easiest card to recommend. It offers a flat 5% cashback on eligible online purchases without restricting you to a specific merchant.

  • Online cashback: 5% (capped at ₹2,000 per statement cycle)

  • Spend requirement to hit the cap: ₹40,000 per statement cycle

  • Offline cashback: 1% (capped at ₹2,000 per statement cycle)

  • Total cashback cap: ₹4,000 per statement cycle

  • Annual fee: ₹999 (waived on ₹2 lakh annual spend) 

  • Exclusions: utilities, insurance, fuel, rent, wallet loading, education, jewellery, railways, government payments, tolls, digital gaming

The online cashback is capped at ₹2,000 per statement cycle, which is reached at ₹40,000 of eligible online spend.Cashback earned on offline purchases has a separate cap, allowing you to earn up to ₹4,000 in total cashback per cycle. Spending beyond ₹40,000 online in the same cycle earns no further online cashback.

Want a detailed breakdown of its rewards, exclusions and recent changes? Read our SBI Cashback Credit Card Review 2026. or if you've already decided it's the right fit, apply for the SBI Cashback Credit Card here. 

2. PhonePe SBI Card SELECT BLACK

PhoonePe SBI card is best suited for users who already use PhonePe and Pincode for regular shopping. If you mainly buy from Amazon, Flipkart and other platforms directly, there are better cashback cards available. 

  • PhonePe/Pincode spends: 10 reward points per ₹100

  • Other online spends: 5% value back

  • Other purchases: 1% value back

  • Other online spend cap: 1,000 reward points/month

  • Annual fee: ₹1,499 (waived at ₹3 lakh annual spend)

The rewards here come as SBI reward points rather than direct cashback, so the value depends on how you redeem them. Unlike SBI Cashback, which credits money to your statement, points must be redeemed through the SBI rewards catalogue. Rewards on other online spends are capped at 1,000 reward points a month, roughly ₹1,000 in value.

3. Jupiter Edge+ CSB Bank Credit Card

Unlike a traditional cashback credit card, the Jupiter Edge+ CSB Bank Credit Card rewards users with Jewels on eligible UPI transactions made through the Jupiter app. That means you'll only get the advertised rewards if your shopping fits the eligible categories and merchants. 

  • Shopping Reward Cap: 10,000 Jewels per billing cycle (3,000 per merchant and 1,000 per transaction) 

  • Travel Jewels (Jupiter Flights): 5,000 Jewels per cycle

  • Redemption value: 1 Jewel = ₹0.20 as vouchers or digital gold; 1 Jewel = ₹0.14 as statement credit

  • Joining fee: ₹0 under current limited-period offer, ₹999 otherwise

  • Annual fee: generally lifetime free

The card is generally offered lifetime free, so most users pay no annual fee. If you're specifically looking to avoid annual fees, you can also compare it with other options in our guide to the best lifetime free credit cards in India. 

Before You Apply

There are a few things to keep in mind before choosing this card:

  • Rewards are earned only on eligible UPI transactions made through the Jupiter app. Regular card payments don't earn Shopping Jewels.

  • Shopping must be your selected reward category and the merchant must be on the eligible list.

  • Shopping rewards are capped at 1,000 Jewels per transaction, 3,000 Jewels per merchant and 10,000 Jewels per billing cycle.

  • Jewels are worth ₹0.20 each when redeemed for eligible vouchers or digital gold, but only ₹0.14 each as statement credit.

  • Gift cards, utility bills, insurance and subscriptions are excluded from the shopping category.

How the Cashback Works

Suppose you spend ₹70,000 online in a statement month:

First ₹5,000 → 2% = ₹100

Next ₹35,000 → 5% = ₹1,750

Remaining ₹30,000 → 7% = ₹2,100

Total cashback = ₹3,950, which remains within the monthly cashback cap of ₹4,000. 

This card makes the most sense if your eligible online spending regularly exceeds ₹40,000 a month. Otherwise, the simpler flat cashback structure of the SBI Cashback Card may offer better overall value.

5. Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card

If Flipkart and Myntra are where you shop most, this card is one of the strongest options available. It offers higher cashback on both platforms than a general cashback card, but the advantage disappears if most of your shopping happens elsewhere. 

  • Myntra: 7.5% cashback (capped at ₹4,000 per statement quarter)

  • Flipkart cashback: 5% (capped at ₹4,000 per statement quarter)

  • Cleartrip cashback: 5% (capped at ₹4,000 per statement quarter)

  • Preferred merchants: unlimited 4% (Swiggy food delivery, Uber, PVR, Cultfit and more)

  • Other spends: unlimited 1% cashback

  • Joining fee: ₹500

  • Annual fee: ₹500 from year two, waived at ₹3.5 lakh annual spend

Flipkart purchases earn 5% cashback (capped at ₹4,000 per statement quarter), while Myntra purchases earn 7.5% cashback (capped at ₹4,000 per statement quarter). 

Preferred merchants such as Swiggy Food, Uber, PVR and Cultfit earn an unlimited 4% cashback, with the Swiggy benefit applicable only on food delivery. 

For example, a ₹60,000 Flipkart purchase earns ₹3,000 cashback, leaving only ₹1,000 cashback available under the quarterly Flipkart cap. 

The card may also offer rotating Swiggy discounts, but these are promotional and should not be treated as permanent card benefits. If you mostly shop on Flipkart or Myntra, apply for the Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card here. 

Every card in this list has its strengths but the best choice ultimately depends on your shopping habits. 

The right credit card should fit the way you already spend, not force you to change your habits. By comparing cashback rates, reward caps, annual fees and exclusions, you can choose a card that delivers consistent value throughout the year. Frequently Asked Questions

1. Which is the best credit card for online shopping in India?

The SBI Cashback Card is one of the best credit cards for online shopping in India for most users because it offers a flat 5% cashback on eligible online purchases across a wide range of merchants. 

However, if you mostly shop on Flipkart or Myntra, use PhonePe frequently or spend more than ₹40,000 online every month, a platform-specific card like Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card, PhonePe SBI Card SELECT BLACK or Axis Bank Cashback Credit Card may offer better value.

2. Which credit card is best for online shopping without an annual fee?

If you want a credit card for online shopping without an annual fee, the Jupiter Edge+ CSB Bank Credit Card is one of the strongest options because it is generally offered lifetime free.

Keep in mind that rewards are earned only on eligible UPI transactions made through the Jupiter app and are paid as Jewels instead of direct cashback. If you're comfortable paying an annual fee, cards like SBI Cashback may still generate higher overall savings.

3. Which credit card is best for Amazon shopping?

For shoppers who frequently buy from Amazon, the SBI Cashback Card is one of the strongest options because it provides 5% cashback on eligible online purchases without restricting rewards to a specific marketplace. 

4. Which credit card is best for Flipkart shopping?

The Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card is designed specifically for Flipkart users, offering 5% cashback on Flipkart and 7.5% cashback on Myntra, subject to quarterly cashback caps. 

5. Which credit card is best for Swiggy?

The answer depends on how you use Swiggy. 

If you're ordering food, the Flipkart Axis Bank Credit Card offers 4% cashback on eligible Swiggy Food orders as a preferred merchant benefit. 

If you're shopping on Swiggy Instamart or other online platforms, the SBI Cashback Card may provide better overall value because of its flat cashback structure.
