HSBC Spend Fest 2026: Rewards, Eligibility, Registration & Offer Details

HSBC Spend Fest 2026 lets eligible HSBC Credit Card holders earn Amazon, MakeMyTrip and Croma or Reliance Digital vouchers by meeting spending milestones between 9 July and 16 August 2026. Register before 16 August 2026 to earn rewards on top of your HSBC Credit Card's regular benefits, provided you meet the eligible spending requirements.

Who Should Consider HSBC Spend Fest?

HSBC Spend Fest 2026 is worth considering if you already have large planned expenses during the offer period such as electronics, furniture, home appliances or travel bookings. Since the rewards are offered in addition to your regular credit card benefits, eligible purchases can help you earn extra value without changing your spending habits.

If you're looking for cards that maximise travel rewards beyond limited-time campaigns, you can also explore our guide to Travel Loyalty Programs Explained: How to Earn Free Flights and Hotel Stays in India (2026).  

Before planning large purchases, make sure the merchant category is eligible. Transactions such as fuel, insurance, utility payments and wallet loads do not count towards the spending milestones.

Who Can Participate?

To qualify, you must:

  • Registration is mandatory. You must register on or before 16 August 2026 to participate in the campaign. 

  • Pay at least the minimum amount due on your HSBC Credit Card.

  • Keep your HSBC Credit Card account active until reward fulfilment. Closed, cancelled or terminated card accounts between 9 July 2026 and 10 December 2026 will not qualify for the offer. 

The offer is available on valid HSBC Credit Cards issued to existing Indian resident customers, except the following cards: 

  • HSBC Taj Credit Card

  • HSBC Premier Credit Card

  • HSBC Prive Credit Card

  • HSBC Corporate Credit Cards

Add-on cardholders do not receive separate rewards. However, eligible spends made on an add-on card are added to the primary cardholder's spending.

How to Register

You must register on or before 16 August 2026 using either of the following methods:

  • Option 1: SMS HFEST to 575750 from your registered mobile number.

  • Option 2: Register through HSBC's campaign page.

Registrations submitted after 16 August 2026 will not be accepted. 

Transactions That Do Not Count

Not every transaction made on your HSBC Credit Card counts towards the campaign. HSBC calculates rewards using net eligible spends, meaning refunded or reversed transactions are deducted and several merchant categories are excluded altogether.

The following transactions are excluded from eligible spending:

  • Utility payments

  • Fuel purchases

  • Insurance payments

  • Tax payments

  • Jewellery purchases

  • E-wallet loads

  • Money transfers

  • Real estate transactions

  • Financial institution transactions

  • Non-financial institution transactions

  • Balance transfer

  • Cash on EMI

  • Passenger railway transactions

  • Collection agency transactions

  • Gambling transactions

  • Home improvement transactions (MCC 7349)

Not sure how Merchant Category Codes work? Read our guide on How Merchant Categories Affect Your Credit Card Rewards in India to understand why some transactions earn rewards while others don't. 

Important Conditions

Keep these points in mind before participating:

  • Only net eligible spends qualify for rewards.

  • Refunded or reversed transactions reduce your eligible spending.

  • Spending across multiple HSBC Credit Cards cannot be combined.

  • You can qualify for only one reward slab based on your highest eligible spend.

  • HSBC may review card usage during the campaign and disqualify accounts in cases of suspicious transactions, unusual spending patterns or misuse of the offer. 

  • Rewards cannot be exchanged for cash, cashback or any alternative reward option after issuance. 

  • The HSBC Spend Fest reward is offered in addition to the regular rewards available on your HSBC Credit Card. 

  • Any applicable tax liability related to the reward will be borne by the cardholder. 

Can You Earn Multiple Rewards?

HSBC awards only one reward based on the highest applicable milestone achieved. If you qualify for multiple milestones, rewards are processed according to the priority order mentioned in the offer terms, starting from the ₹50 lakh milestone.

Is HSBC Spend Fest 2026 Worth It? 

HSBC Spend Fest 2026 can be a valuable limited-time offer if you already have large planned purchases and an eligible HSBC Credit Card. Before chasing the spending milestones, make sure your transactions qualify under HSBC's eligible merchant categories, register on time for the campaign and remember that only the highest reward slab is awarded.

FAQs

1. Is registration mandatory for HSBC Spend Fest 2026?

Yes. You must register before 16 August 2026 by sending HFEST to 575750 from your registered mobile number or through HSBC's official campaign page. 

2. Which HSBC Credit Cards are not eligible?

The offer does not apply to HSBC Taj, HSBC Premier, HSBC Prive or HSBC Corporate Credit Cards.

3. Which transactions are excluded from HSBC Spend Fest?

Fuel, utility, insurance, tax, jewellery, e-wallet, money transfer and several other Merchant Category Code (MCC) categories are excluded.

4. Can I combine spends across multiple HSBC Credit Cards?

No. Spending on multiple HSBC Credit Cards cannot be combined to reach a reward milestone. However, eligible spends made on an add-on card are added to the primary cardholder's spending for the same card account.

5. When will HSBC send the rewards?

Eligible customers are expected to receive their vouchers through SMS or email by 10 December 2026 after HSBC completes reward verification.
