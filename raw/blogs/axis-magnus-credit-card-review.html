Axis Magnus Credit Card Review

Among India’s premium credit cards, Axis Magnus is built for users who spend high amounts every month and want travel-led value. It offers airport lounge access, EDGE Reward Points, Travel EDGE rewards, dining benefits, hotel offers and airline or hotel transfer options. But given this is not a simple cashback card - your spending, travel usage and redemption style can raise the value of this card for you. 

Quick Answer

Axis Magnus can still make sense for high spenders who regularly cross ₹2 lakh in eligible monthly spends, travel often, use lounge access and know how to redeem EDGE Reward Points through airline or hotel partners.

Eligibility Criteria

Basic eligibility:

  • Primary cardholder must be between 18 and 70 years of age

  • Add-on cardholder must be at least 18 years old

  • Applicant must be a resident of India

  • Salaried applicants should have a net salary of ₹24 lakh per annum or self-employed applicants should have a net annual ITR of ₹24 lakh. 

Note: Meeting the eligibility criteria doesn't guarantee approval. Axis Bank also considers internal credit policies, repayment history and your overall credit profile before issuing the card. 

Fees, Charges and Welcome Benefits

The joining and renewal fees are both equal at ₹12,500 + 18% GST. So the first year is not cheaper than the later years. 

Getting the card may include some welcome benefits, where cardholders can choose one option from the available voucher choices:

  • Domestic flight ticket

  • Luxe Gift Card

  • Postcard Hotels voucher

  • Yatra voucher

The welcome voucher can roughly offset the joining fee in year one but only if the voucher category suits your actual usage. A voucher is not the same as cash, so its real value depends on whether you were going to use that brand or service anyway.

The annual fee can be waived off at ₹25 lakh eligible spend in the preceding card year however not every transaction counts toward this target. 

The excluded categories include: 

  • Gold and jewellery

  • Fuel

  • Wallet reloads or wallet payments

  • Utilities

  • Government payments

  • Insurance

  • EMI conversions

  • Cash withdrawals

  • Refunded, reversed or cancelled transactions

  • Fees and charges

If you're unfamiliar with how merchant categories affect rewards and fee waivers, it's worth reading How Merchant Categories Affect Your Credit Card Rewards in India. 

How Does the Reward Structure Work? 

This is where Axis Magnus becomes interesting.

Unlike many premium credit cards that offer a flat reward rate, Magnus rewards users differently depending on how much they spend every month.

Higher monthly spending unlocks significantly better earning rates.

The 35 EDGE Reward point rate does not apply endlessly. It is capped at eligible monthly spend equal to your credit limit plus ₹1.5 lakh. Any spend above that cap goes back to the base earn rate.

For example, if you spend ₹2 lakh in eligible categories in one month:

  • First ₹1.5 lakh earns around 9,000 EDGE Reward Points

  • Next ₹50,000 earns around 8,750 EDGE Reward Points

  • Total comes to around 17,750 EDGE Reward Points before redemption or transfer

EDGE Reward Points Transfer Program

For many users, this is where the Axis Magnus Credit Card delivers its biggest value. EDGE Reward Points can be transferred to airline and hotel loyalty programs through Axis Bank’s Travel EDGE platform

For most transfer partners, the current transfer ratio is: 

5 EDGE Reward Points = 2 partner miles or points

Some partners transfer at a weaker 5:1 ratio, so checking the ratio before transferring is important.

Axis Bank also applies annual transfer limits based on partner groups.

2026 Transfer Partner Changes

Axis removed Accor, Marriott Bonvoy and Qatar Airways Privilege Club from the transfer partner list in April 2026. This matters if you were planning to use Magnus mainly for those hotel or airline programs.

Axis has also added IndiGo BluChip as a transfer partner, giving Magnus users another domestic airline redemption option. This can help frequent IndiGo flyers, but users should still check the current transfer ratio and redemption value before moving EDGE Reward Points.

Airport Lounge Access

Airport lounge access continues to be one of the strongest benefits of the Axis Magnus Credit Card, especially for frequent travellers. However, unlike before, domestic lounge access now comes with a spending requirement. 

To enjoy complimentary domestic lounge access, you must spend at least ₹50,000 in the previous three calendar months. 

Other important lounge rules include:

  • Unlimited domestic lounge access at select lounges

  • New cardholders usually get relaxation for the month of issuance and the next 3 calendar months

  • Unlimited international lounge access is available through Priority Pass

  • 4 complimentary guest visits are available per year

Other Travel and Lifestyle Benefits

The card also includes a few useful travel, dining and protection benefits such as:

  • 2% Forex markup 

  • Up to 30% off at 4,000+ restaurants through Axis Dining Delights

  • 1% fuel surcharge waiver on transactions between ₹400 and ₹4,000

  • Fuel surcharge waiver capped at ₹400 per statement cycle

  • Zero cash withdrawal fee

Note: Zero cash withdrawal fee does not mean cash withdrawals are free. Interest starts from day one at 3% per month, so cash withdrawal should usually be avoided.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Applying only because the card has premium positioning

  • Assuming all spends earn EDGE Reward Points

  • Forgetting that fee waiver spends and statement spends are different

  • Ignoring the ₹50,000 domestic lounge spend condition

  • Treating catalogue redemption as the main use case

  • Transferring points without checking award availability

  • Assuming Accor, Marriott or Qatar Airways are still available

  • Using the card heavily for excluded categories

  • Withdrawing cash because the cash advance fee is nil

  • Paying interest and wiping out reward value

Is the Axis Magnus Credit Card Still Worth It in 2026?

Yes, but only for the right type of user.

Axis Magnus is still a strong premium travel card, but it is not for every premium credit card user. It works best for people who regularly spend around ₹2 lakh or more per month in eligible categories, travel often, use lounge access and understand how to redeem EDGE Reward Points through airline or hotel partners.

The card becomes more useful when your spends naturally cross the ₹1.5 lakh monthly level, because that is when the higher reward rate starts applying. If you can also use the ₹25 lakh fee waiver or recover the annual cost through meaningful travel redemptions, the value can still make sense.

However, if your monthly eligible spend is moderate, you mainly want cashback, or you do not actively use travel rewards, the ₹12,500 + GST annual fee becomes harder to justify. The card also needs more tracking now because reward exclusions, lounge conditions, transfer ratios and partner changes can directly affect your final value.

Ultimately, Axis Magnus is not just about how many points you earn. Its real value depends on how well you redeem those points.

FAQs

Is The Axis Magnus Credit Card worth it in 2026?

It is worth it for users who regularly spend above ₹2 lakh per month in eligible categories and actively use airline or hotel transfer partners. For users below that level, or those who prefer cashback, the ₹14,750 annual cost is difficult to recover.

What is the Axis Magnus transfer ratio?

Most airline and hotel partners currently support transfers at 5 EDGE Reward Points = 2 partner miles or points, although some partners have different ratios. Always check the latest transfer rates before initiating a transfer. 

What is the lounge access Eligibility for Axis Magnus?

Domestic lounge access requires ₹50,000 eligible spends in the previous 3 calendar months. International lounge access is available through Priority Pass, with 4 complimentary guest visits per year.

How does the ₹25 lakh fee waiver work?

The fee waiver is based on eligible spends in the preceding card year, not total statement spends. Fuel, utilities, wallet loads, insurance, government payments, EMI conversions and rent transactions are generally excluded.

Has Axis Magnus been devalued? 

Yes. Over the past few years, Axis Bank has revised the reward structure, introduced spend-based conditions for certain benefits, and removed popular transfer partners such as Accor Live Limitless, Marriott Bonvoy, and Qatar Airways Privilege Club.
