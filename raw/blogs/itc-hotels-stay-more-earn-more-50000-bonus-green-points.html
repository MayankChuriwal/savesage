How to Earn 50,000 Bonus Green Points with ITC Hotels

Planning a few hotel stays over the next few months? ITC Hotels has a new promotion that can get you up to 50,000 Bonus Green Points if you stay across its different hotel brands. The offer is valid from 21 August 2026 to 20 February 2027. Just keep in mind that you need to complete at least 10 eligible room nights during this period to qualify for any bonus.

24 Aug 2026

How Does the ITC Hotels Offer Work? 

The idea is pretty straightforward. Stay at different ITC hotels or across different brands and you can unlock a bigger bonus as you move up the tiers:

  • 3 different hotels OR 2 different brands: 2,000 Bonus Green Points

  • 4 different hotels OR 3 different brands: 5,000 Bonus Green Points

  • 5 different hotels OR 4 different brands: 20,000 Bonus Green Points

  • 5 different brands: 50,000 Bonus Green Points

One important thing: You need at least 10 eligible room nights in total during the offer period to qualify for any Bonus Green Points. You don't need to stay a fixed number of nights at each hotel. For example, you could stay 1 night each at four different hotels and 6 nights at a fifth hotel. As long as the stays are eligible, that adds up to the required 10 room nights.

The five participating brands are ITC Hotels, Welcomhotel, Mementos, Storii and Fortune Hotels. So, if your goal is the maximum 50,000 Bonus Green Points, you need to stay across all five participating brands while also meeting the 10 eligible room-night requirement. 

Are ITC Hotels Bonus Green Points Cumulative?

No, the regular Green Points you earn on eligible stays are separate from the Bonus Green Points. The Bonus Green Points don't stack across tiers, so you’ll receive only the highest bonus tier you qualify for. For example, if you reach the 50,000-point tier, you’ll get 50,000 Bonus Green Points, not 57,000 points by adding all four tiers together. 

ITC Hotels Stay More Earn More Offer Terms 

Before you start booking, there are a few things worth knowing:

  • Your booking needs to be made through the official ITC Hotels website or app. Bookings through other channels won't qualify.

  • You need to complete at least 10 eligible room nights between 21 August 2026 and 20 February 2027, with both dates included, to qualify for the Bonus Green Points. 

  • Your regular Green Points will be credited after check-out.

  • Your Bonus Green Points will be credited within 30 days after the offer period ends.

So, if you already have a few ITC stays planned, this is a nice opportunity to pick up some extra Green Points. If you're aiming for a bigger bonus, try spreading your stays across different hotels or brands to move up the reward tiers.
