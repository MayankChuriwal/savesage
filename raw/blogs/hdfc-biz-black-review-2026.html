HDFC Biz Black Credit Card Review 2026

HDFC Biz Black is worth considering if you regularly spend above ₹50,000 per month on eligible business categories like GST, Income Tax, utilities, travel or business tools. It is not the right card if your spends are low, irregular, mostly excluded or dependent on platforms where Diners Club acceptance is limited.

Who Can Apply for HDFC Biz Black?

HDFC Biz Black is mainly built for self-employed Indian citizens and business owners aged 21 to 65 with an annual ITR above ₹30 lakh. HDFC does not mention a fixed minimum credit score, but weak credit history or past defaults can hurt approval chances.

Before applying, it is useful to keep your ITR, GST returns, bank statements and other business-income documents ready, since HDFC will still evaluate the application based on its internal checks. This card is not meant for students, low spenders or salaried users without business spends. 

Fees and Welcome Benefits

The HDFC Biz Black Credit Card has a joining fee of ₹10,000 + GST. This joining fee can be waived if you spend ₹1.5 lakh within the first 90 days of getting the card.

If you meet this spend condition, you can also receive a Club Marriott membership and a ₹5,000 Taj Stay voucher. However, this welcome benefit is generally meant for eligible new cardholders and may not apply in the same way to conversions, migrations or upgrades.

The renewal fee is also ₹10,000 + GST. It can be waived if you spend ₹7.5 lakh or more in the previous card anniversary year. This fee structure makes sense only if your business already has regular card-payable expenses, not if you need to create extra spends just to avoid the fee.

How Much Are HDFC Biz Black Reward Points Worth?

HDFC Biz Black earns Reward Points when you spend on eligible card transactions. These points are not automatic cashback; their value depends on how you redeem them.

The best value usually comes from eligible flight and hotel bookings through HDFC SmartBuy, where 1 Reward Point can be worth up to ₹1. Points can typically be used for up to 70 percent of the booking value. Poor redemption choices can reduce the actual value.

Base Rewards

The card earns 5 Reward Points per ₹150 on eligible regular spends, giving around 3.3% value at ₹1 per point. However, categories like petrol, wallet loads, rent, education and EMI transactions may be excluded from core rewards.

5X Rewards: Where Biz Black Becomes Powerful

The main attraction of HDFC Biz Black is its 5X reward structure. On regular eligible spends, the card earns 5 Reward Points per ₹150. On eligible 5X business categories, it earns 25 Reward Points per ₹150, which can give around 16.6% value if each point is redeemed at ₹1 for eligible travel bookings.

These 5X categories are useful because they are linked to real business expenses, not just lifestyle spends. They include eligible payments across:

  • Income Tax and advance tax

  • GST payments

  • utility and bill payments through PayZapp or SmartPay

  • MMT MyBiz bookings through SmartBuy BizDeals

  • business tools such as Tally, Office 365, AWS, Google Workspace, Azure and Credflow through eligible platforms

However, the 5X benefit does not apply just because a payment looks like a business expense. Some categories such as rent, education, wallet loads, FASTag, municipal tax, loans, credit card payments and certain other spends may not qualify. Before making a large payment only for rewards, check whether that transaction is actually eligible under HDFC’s latest terms.

To unlock accelerated rewards, you need ₹50,000 or more spends in a statement cycle. The 5X rate is made up of the normal 5 Reward Points per ₹150 plus an extra 20 Reward Points per ₹150. This extra 20 Reward Points portion is the bonus part and it is capped at 7,500 Reward Points per statement cycle.

Because the bonus portion is 20 Reward Points per ₹150, the 7,500-point bonus cap is reached at around ₹56,250 of eligible 5X spends. So the card can be very rewarding, but not unlimited. If your eligible 5X spends go much beyond this level, the extra spends may not earn the same high accelerated value.

SmartBuy Benefits

SmartBuy is HDFC’s rewards and travel platform. Biz Black can earn up to 10X Reward Points on select retail categories and 5X on eligible business travel or tools through SmartBuy BizDeals. Both benefits use the same 10,000 Reward Points monthly cap, so check current category terms before planning spends.

Milestone Benefits

Biz Black gives a ₹5,000 Flight or Taj Stay voucher on every ₹5 lakh spent, excluding categories such as EMI, rent and petrol.

  • ₹5 lakh annual spends: ₹5,000 voucher

  • ₹10 lakh annual spends: ₹10,000 voucher

  • ₹15 lakh annual spends: ₹15,000 voucher

  • ₹20 lakh annual spends: ₹20,000 voucher

Lounge and Travel Benefits

HDFC Biz Black offers unlimited domestic and international airport lounge access for eligible active cards, including both primary and eligible add-on cardholders. This can be useful for business owners who travel frequently or have add-on cards for family or business use.

The card also has a 2% forex markup on foreign currency transactions. This is lower than many cards that charge around 3.5%, but it is still a cost. International spends make sense only if the transaction earns rewards and you redeem those points well.

The Diners Club Problem

HDFC Biz Black runs on the Diners Club network. At large hotel chains, airlines, premium merchants and many online platforms, acceptance is usually fine. But two major business-spend platforms do not work with Diners Club: Google Ads and Meta Ads.

Some smaller merchants and local vendors may also decline Diners Club. Before applying, check whether your main vendors, ad platforms, travel portals, software tools and suppliers actually accept Diners Club.

Other Business Benefits

The card includes a business insurance package at an annual premium of ₹3,785 including GST, with cover for shop fire and burglary up to ₹5 lakh, cash in safe or transit up to ₹25,000, electronic equipment up to ₹50,000 and hospital cash benefit up to ₹1,000 per day. It also offers up to 55 days of interest-free credit if dues are paid in full.

Pros and Cons

Pros

  • Strong 5X rewards on eligible business spends, with up to around 16.6% value at best travel redemption.

  • Good base reward rate of around 3.3% on eligible spends.

  • Rewards on useful business categories such as GST, Income Tax and utilities.

  • Fee waiver options can reduce the cost if your spends are naturally high.

  • Milestone vouchers can add up to ₹20,000 value annually.

  • Unlimited lounge access can be useful for frequent travellers.

Cons

  • High annual fee of ₹10,000 + GST if you cannot use the benefits properly.

  • 5X rewards need ₹50,000 or more statement-cycle spends to unlock accelerated rewards.

  • Bonus reward cap applies, so the highest reward rate is not unlimited.

  • Diners Club acceptance can be limited, and Google Ads / Meta Ads may not work.

  • Highest value depends on travel redemption, so poor redemption choices reduce the real return.

  • Not suitable for low or irregular spenders because the fee may not be justified.

Before paying a ₹10,000 + GST premium card fee, compare your actual monthly business spends against the card’s eligible categories, caps, exclusions and redemption value. SaveSage helps you check whether the rewards work for your real expenses, not just the headline rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is HDFC Biz Black worth the ₹10,000 annual fee?
Yes, if you naturally spend ₹50,000 or more per month on eligible business categories and redeem points well.

Can I use HDFC Biz Black for Google Ads or Meta Ads?
No. Since HDFC Biz Black runs on the Diners Club network, Google Ads and Meta Ads do not accept this card. That means those spends will not earn rewards or help you meet the card’s spend conditions.

What is the 5X reward capping on HDFC Biz Black?
The bonus portion is capped at 7,500 Reward Points per statement cycle, reached at around ₹56,250 of eligible 5X spends.

Who is eligible for HDFC Biz Black?
It is mainly for self-employed Indian citizens and business owners aged 21 to 65 with annual ITR above ₹30 lakh.

How can I waive the HDFC Biz Black annual fee?
The joining fee is waived on ₹1.5 lakh spends in 90 days, and the renewal fee is waived on ₹7.5 lakh annual spends.
